Civil and structural engineering professional with over 44 years of hands-on leadership managing city-wide water supply networks, large water and wastewater treatment plants, seawater desalination facilities, and industrial wastewater recycling schemes.

As former Engineering Director and Chief Engineer at CMWSSB, held complete operational responsibility for drinking water transmission and distribution across the Chennai metropolitan area, serving over 10 million residents daily. Commanded raw water drawals from major reservoirs, treatment across massive facilities (including the 530 MLD Chembarambakkam WTP, Kilpauk, and Puzhal), and managed daily distribution balances across all zonal reservoirs.

Industrial Circular Economy & Drought Resilience
Successfully executed landmark multi-crore utility projects, including two 45 MLD Tertiary Treatment Reverse Osmosis (TTRO) plants supplying recycled water to manufacturing corridors in Sriperumbudur and Irungattukottai through dedicated high-pressure pipelines. During the critical 2017 drought, mobilized an unconventional emergency source by testing and pumping raw water from 22 abandoned stone quarries at Sikkarayapuram directly to Chembarambakkam WTP, feeding 15 MLD of treated potable water into city mains.

Currently serves as Senior Advisor to Bayoh Sustainability Solutions (BSS), Chennai, providing technology support, process evaluation, and project acquisition for turnkey water and wastewater schemes (ETP/ZLD/STP) and institutional Green/Energy Audits. Concurrently advises VVV Projects & Systems on the comprehensive refurbishing and structural rehabilitation of the 100 MLD Seawater Desalination Plant at Minjur, and leads climate-resilient decentralized infrastructure as Vice-Chairman at Wasmanpro Environmental Solutions.
